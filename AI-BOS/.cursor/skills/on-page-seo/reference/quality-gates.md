# On-Page SEO Quality Gates

## Blocking

- Money pages without documented primary intent
- Duplicate H1 or title patterns at scale on indexable URLs
- YMYL pages without E-E-A-T plan
- Orphan indexable revenue pages

## Non-Blocking

- Meta length polish
- Extra internal link suggestions
- Secondary keyword opportunities

## Severity

Critical → High → Medium → Low → Informational

Each finding: page, evidence, handbook ref, fix, validation.
