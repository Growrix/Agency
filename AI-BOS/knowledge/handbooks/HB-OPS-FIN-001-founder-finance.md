# Founder Finance Handbook — Pricing and Unit Economics

## Purpose

Advisory framing for pricing, unit economics, and revenue models for digital businesses — not a bookkeeping product.

## Scope

Pricing tiers, service packages, digital product pricing, rough COGS/margin thinking, break-even estimates. Human owns accounting, tax, invoicing tools.

## Principles

1. **Advisory only** — no payment processing or ledger software by AI.
2. **Tie to offer** — pricing follows offer from marketing/sales handbooks.
3. **Conservative assumptions** — label estimates explicitly.

## Standards

### Models to evaluate

| Model | When |
|-------|------|
| Service packages | Agency / done-for-you |
| One-time digital product | Templates, courses, assets |
| SaaS subscription | Recurring software |
| Hybrid | Service + product upsell |

### Output

- Pricing table draft (MD)
- Unit economics assumptions (human validates)
- Memory record type `finance`

## Anti-patterns

- Inventing revenue numbers without labels
- Replacing accountant or Stripe dashboard
