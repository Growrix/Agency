# Social Media Strategy Handbook

## Purpose

Define organic social strategy, platform mix, cadence, and platform-native content playbooks for Marketing OS engagements.

## Scope

Social manager and social writer agents. Paid social creative briefs hand off to Paid Ads division; community moderation plans to Community division.

## Principles

1. **Platform-native** — format, length, and hook differ per channel; no cross-post identical blobs.
2. **Cadence over virality** — sustainable posting beats sporadic spikes.
3. **Engagement loops** — every post ties to comment/DM strategy or next content piece.
4. **Human publishes** — agents deliver ready posts; human owns accounts and scheduling tools.

## Standards

### Strategy deliverables

- Platform selection matrix (where ICP actually spends time)
- Content mix ratio (education, proof, offer, culture)
- Weekly cadence table with post types
- Hashtag/keyword conventions per platform

### Platform playbooks (minimum)

| Platform | Formats | Spec notes |
|----------|---------|------------|
| LinkedIn | Text, carousel brief, document post | B2B thought leadership, 1–2 posts/day max recommendation |
| Instagram | Reel script, carousel, story sequence | Visual-first; link in bio strategy |
| Facebook | Community posts, event promos | Group vs page strategy |
| X/Twitter | Threads, single posts | Real-time engagement windows |
| YouTube Shorts | Hook-first scripts | Delegate long-form to Video division |

### Writer deliverables

- Post copy with character counts
- First-comment strategy where applicable
- UTM-tagged link placeholders
- Asset brief for creative division (thumbnail, carousel slides)

### Storage

`projects/<slug>/marketing/social/` — `strategy.md`, `playbooks/`, `posts/`, `calendars/`

## Best Practices

- Batch 2 weeks of posts per approval cycle for human efficiency.
- Repurpose from content division pillars — don't invent parallel themes.
- Track post ids in scorecard when analytics access exists.

## Anti-patterns

- Advising buy-followers or engagement pods
- Identical copy across LinkedIn and Instagram
- Social strategy without defined offer or landing destination
