# Founder Research Workflow

## Purpose

Repeatable research flow from question to grounded brief in founder memory.

## Standards

```text
1. INTAKE — question, market, competitors from founder or inbox
2. GROUND — memory + MC-KNW-RETRIEVE / registry
3. RESEARCH — WebSearch/WebFetch; MC-PLT-CODEBASE if technical
4. SYNTHESIZE — brief MD + JSON record in founder-os-memory/
5. GATE — present findings; human approves direction
6. HANDOFF — @founder-os (plan) or @founder-marketing (offer)
```
